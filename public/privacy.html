3. Android Device Permissions & Technical Disclosures

In accordance with Google Play's Prominent Disclosure and Consent requirements, our app requests the following system permissions solely to perform designated automotive functions:

  • Bluetooth Permissions (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_ADMIN):
    Required to search for, pair with, and maintain serial data communication with your wireless ELM327 / OBDLink Bluetooth vehicle diagnostic dongle plugged into your car's OBD-II port. Bluetooth is never used to track physical user location.
  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION on legacy Android):
    On Android versions 11 and earlier, Android system architecture grouped Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) scanning under location permissions. Our app does NOT record, log, trace, or upload your physical GPS coordinates or travel history.
  • Microphone Permission (RECORD_AUDIO):
    Requested only when you explicitly launch the engine sound diagnostic module. Audio samples are analyzed in volatile memory for harmonic sound peak detection and are immediately discarded upon completion of the test.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
    Used to alert drivers to critical mechanical anomalies (e.g., Engine Coolant > 108°C, Low DEF/AdBlue levels) and subscription status receipts. You can disable notifications at any time in Android Settings.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE):
    Required to fetch AI mechanical fault code definitions, vehicle maintenance recall bulletins, regional repair labor cost tables, and manage authentication.
